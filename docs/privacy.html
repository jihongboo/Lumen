Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026.

Information Collection and Use

Lumen Noise is built with privacy as a priority. We believe that your personal information belongs to you.

Location Data

Lumen Noise requests access to your device's location to provide local weather information and adapt the ambient display accordingly. This location data is processed locally on your device and sent directly to weather service providers (such as Apple Weather) to retrieve current conditions. We do not store or track your location history on our servers.

No Account Required

You can use Lumen Noise without creating an account. We do not collect personal identifiers such as your name, email address, or phone number unless you explicitly provide them to us when contacting support.

Usage Data

We do not collect analytics or usage data about how you use the app. Your settings and preferences are stored locally on your device and, if enabled, synced via your private iCloud account.

Data Security

Because we collect very little data, there is very little to protect. Any data synced via iCloud is protected by Apple's security infrastructure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@example.com.
