Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Lumen Noise ask for location permission?

Lumen Noise uses location to show local weather and adapt the ambient display. Weather features may be limited if permission is denied.

Why is weather not appearing?

Check that Location Services are enabled for Lumen Noise, then reopen the app. Weather availability can also depend on network access and Apple Weather services.

How do I change the ambient sound?

Use the sound controls in the app to choose rain, ocean, forest, wind, or fire. The selected sound is remembered for future sessions.

Does the app work without sound?

Yes. You can use Lumen Noise as a silent ambient clock and leave sound playback off.

Which devices are supported?

Lumen Noise is designed for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple TV.

Troubleshooting

  • Restart the app if weather or audio does not update after changing permissions.
  • Confirm the device is not muted and volume is turned up when using ambient sound.
  • On Apple TV, use the remote to move focus between display and sound controls.
  • Install the latest app and OS updates if a display or playback issue persists.

Privacy

Lumen Noise uses location only to provide local weather. The app does not require an account and does not include social features. If you disable location access, the app can still be used as an ambient clock.
